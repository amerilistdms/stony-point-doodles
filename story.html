Our story

We aren’t trying to raise the most puppies.

We’re trying to raise our puppies the right way.

Welcome to the Stony Point family

Raised in our home. Loved from day one.

There is a big difference between simply breeding puppies and truly raising them.

Every litter is born and raised inside our home. They hear household sounds, meet people, and grow up surrounded by affection, not a row of kennels.

Our goal is not to produce as many puppies as possible. It is to raise a small number of exceptional puppies and place each one with a family who will love them for the rest of their lives.

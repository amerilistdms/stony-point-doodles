Why We Don’t Always Have Puppies Available

A small breeding program with a big commitment to quality, care, and raising happy, healthy puppies.

Our approach

Quality, care, and personal attention before quantity

One of the questions we hear most often at Stony Point Doodles is, “When will you have more puppies available?”

We love that so many families are interested in welcoming one of our Bernedoodles into their homes. And while we wish we could make everyone’s puppy dreams come true exactly when they’re ready, the reality is that we don’t always have puppies available.

There’s a very good reason for that.

At Stony Point Doodles, we’ve made a conscious decision to remain a small, boutique breeding program where quality, care, and personal attention will always come before quantity.

Who we are

We’re not a large breeding operation, and that’s by choice

There are breeders out there with dozens of breeding females, multiple litters arriving every month, and puppies available almost year round.

That’s simply not who we are.

We don’t have 20, 30, or 50 breeding females. We don’t operate a commercial kennel, and we’re certainly not a puppy mill. We’re a small, family oriented breeding program that believes raising exceptional puppies starts with giving our dogs the love, attention, and care they deserve.

Our dogs are part of our family. They aren’t simply breeding animals, and producing puppies isn’t something we approach like an assembly line.

We believe there’s something special about raising puppies in a home environment, where they receive individual attention, affection, early socialization, and the opportunity to experience everyday family life.

And because we’re small, we’re able to be personally involved in every stage of their development.

Timing

Why we don’t have puppies available all the time

The answer is actually pretty simple: We breed selectively, not constantly.

We carefully plan our litters based on the health, maturity, and well being of our breeding dogs. We don’t breed simply because there’s demand or because someone is ready to purchase a puppy.

Our breeding females are given the time and care they need, and we never want the pressure of having puppies available to dictate our decisions.

That means there may be several months between litters. Sometimes there may be a longer wait.

We understand that this isn’t ideal for everyone, especially families who have decided they’re ready to bring a puppy home right now.

But for us, doing things the right way is more important than doing things quickly.

What matters

The difference between availability and exclusivity

When you’re searching for a Bernedoodle, you’ll likely come across breeders who have puppies available immediately. Some may even offer a large selection of colors, sizes, and ages at any given time.

And while availability can certainly be convenient, we believe choosing a breeder should involve much more than simply finding a puppy that’s ready to go home today.

At Stony Point Doodles, our small program allows us to focus on the things that matter most:

  • Personal attention: Every puppy is raised with love, handling, and individual care.
  • Thoughtful breeding decisions: We prioritize the health, temperament, and overall well being of our dogs.
  • Early socialization: Our puppies grow up surrounded by everyday household activity and human interaction.
  • A personal relationship with our families: We enjoy getting to know the people who will be welcoming our puppies into their lives.
  • A commitment beyond pickup day: We want to remain a resource for our puppy families long after their new companion goes home.

We’re not trying to produce the most puppies. We’re trying to raise puppies we’re proud of.

To us, exclusivity isn’t about charging more or making puppies difficult to obtain. It’s about keeping our program intentionally small so we can give each litter the attention it deserves.

For families

We understand that not everyone can wait

We completely understand the excitement of deciding to bring a puppy into your family.

Maybe you’ve been thinking about it for years. Maybe your children are finally old enough. Perhaps you’ve recently lost a beloved companion and feel ready to open your heart again.

When that moment arrives, waiting another six months or longer can feel like an eternity.

And we understand that some families simply can’t wait.

If that’s the case, we genuinely wish you the very best in finding the right puppy from another responsible, reputable breeder.

But if you’ve fallen in love with our dogs, appreciate the way we raise our puppies, and want to become part of the Stony Point Doodles family, we hope you’ll consider joining our waitlist.

Because sometimes, the things worth having are worth waiting for.

Next steps

How our puppy waitlist works

Since we only have a limited number of litters, our waitlist helps families plan ahead and secure priority when puppies become available.

The process is simple:

  1. Complete our puppy application. This gives us an opportunity to learn more about you, your family, and what you’re looking for in a Bernedoodle.
  2. Receive approval. Once we’ve reviewed and approved your application, we’ll explain the next steps.
  3. Reserve your place. Families who choose to submit a reservation deposit are placed on our priority waitlist in the order their deposits are received.
  4. Stay informed. We’ll share updates about our breeding plans, anticipated litters, and puppy arrivals.
  5. Choose your puppy. When a litter becomes available, families have the opportunity to select a puppy according to their waitlist priority and the puppies available.

We always remind families that breeding involves nature, and even the best plans can change. Litter timing, size, colors, and the number of puppies born can never be guaranteed.

That’s another reason why patience is such an important part of the process.

Take your time

A puppy is a lifelong commitment, not an impulse purchase

When you think about it, a puppy may be part of your family for the next 12 to 15 years, and sometimes even longer.

In the grand scheme of things, waiting a few extra months to find the right puppy from a breeder you trust is a relatively small investment of time.

We encourage families to do their research, ask questions, learn about the breed, and choose a breeder whose values align with their own.

Whether you ultimately choose Stony Point Doodles or another responsible breeding program, we believe that decision should be based on confidence, trust, and the care that goes into raising your future companion.

Not simply who has a puppy available this weekend.

Our measure of success

Small by design. Raised with love.

At Stony Point Doodles, we’re proud to be a small breeding program.

We don’t measure our success by how many litters we produce or how many puppies we place each year.

We measure it by the healthy, happy puppies we raise, the wonderful families we meet, and the joy we get from watching our puppies grow into cherished family companions.

Seeing photos and videos of our puppies thriving in their forever homes is one of the most rewarding parts of what we do.

And that’s something we never want to lose by becoming too big.

We may not always have puppies available, but when we do, you can be sure that each litter has been thoughtfully planned and raised with the love and personal attention that define Stony Point Doodles.

To all the families who have supported us, trusted us, and patiently waited for one of our puppies, thank you. It truly means the world to us.

And to those just discovering Stony Point Doodles, we invite you to follow our journey, get to know our dogs, and consider joining our growing family.

We believe the right puppy is worth the wait. And we hope you will too.

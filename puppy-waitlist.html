How Our Puppy Waitlist Works

A small, boutique breeding program. If you're interested in one of our puppies and are comfortable waiting for the right litter, here's how our waitlist works.

Our approach

Quality over quantity

At Stony Point Doodles, we believe in quality over quantity. We're a small, family based boutique breeding program, and unlike large commercial breeders, we don't breed puppies in volume.

Our litters are thoughtfully planned, with the health, temperament, and well being of our dogs and puppies always coming first. We anticipate having approximately 3 to 4 litters per year, which means there may be times when we don't have puppies immediately available.

We understand that welcoming a puppy into your family is an exciting decision, and we want to make the process as transparent, enjoyable, and stress free as possible.

Step 1

Complete Our Puppy Application

The first step is to complete our puppy application. This helps us learn more about you, your family, your lifestyle, and what you're looking for in your future companion.

We carefully review each application because finding the right homes for our puppies is incredibly important to us.

Complete the application

Step 2

Join Our Waitlist

Once your application is approved, you'll have the option to join our waitlist.

There is no deposit required to join our initial waitlist.

Being on our waitlist simply lets us know you're seriously interested in a future Stony Point Doodles puppy.

We'll keep you informed about our upcoming breeding plans, potential pairings, pregnancy updates, and anticipated litter timelines.

Step 3

Pregnancy Confirmation and Reserving Your Spot

Once we have a confirmed pregnancy and an anticipated due date, we'll reach out to families on our waitlist.

At that point, you'll have the opportunity to submit a $500 deposit to officially reserve your selection priority for that litter.

Your position on the puppy selection list will be determined by the order in which deposits are accepted.

For example, if you're first on the confirmed reservation list, you'll have the first opportunity to select your puppy when selection day arrives.

If the timing isn't right for your family, that's perfectly fine! You can let us know that you'd prefer to wait for a future litter.

Step 4

The Puppies Arrive!

This is where the excitement really begins!

Once the puppies are born, we'll share photos, videos, and regular updates so our families can follow their growth and development.

You'll have the opportunity to get to know the puppies, watch their personalities begin to emerge, and start thinking about which little one might be the perfect fit for your family.

Step 5

Puppy Selection at Approximately 5 to 6 Weeks

We generally begin the official puppy selection process when the puppies are approximately 5 to 6 weeks old, after their veterinary health evaluations and once we have a better understanding of their individual development and personalities.

Selections take place in reservation order.

For example:

  • First pick: The family holding the first selection spot chooses first.
  • Second pick: The family holding the second spot chooses from the remaining available puppies.
  • Third pick: The next family selects, and so on.

This process continues until all available puppies have been selected.

We believe waiting a few weeks before making final selections gives families a better opportunity to choose a puppy based on more than just appearance.

Step 6

Bringing Your Puppy Home

Our puppies are generally ready to join their forever families at approximately 8 weeks of age, provided they are healthy, developmentally ready, and cleared by our veterinarian.

We'll coordinate pickup arrangements and provide information to help you prepare for your puppy's arrival.

Our goal is to make the transition into your home as smooth and happy as possible.

Why We Do Things Differently

We know that waiting for a puppy isn't for everyone, and that's okay!

At Stony Point Doodles, we're not focused on producing as many puppies as possible. We're focused on raising healthy, happy, well socialized puppies and matching them with loving families.

Because our program is intentionally small, our availability can be limited, and our breeding plans may change based on the health and well being of our dogs.

We believe the right puppy is worth waiting for.

If you appreciate a more personal breeding experience, where you can follow your puppy's journey from the planning stages through the day you bring them home, we'd love to welcome you to the Stony Point Doodles family.

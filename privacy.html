Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 · Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Stony Point Doodles respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how Stony Point Doodles, referred to as “Stony Point Doodles,” “we,” “us,” or “our,” collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you visit our website, submit a puppy application, contact us, reserve or purchase a puppy, join a mailing list, or otherwise interact with us.

By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Mailing address
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Information submitted through our contact forms
  • Information submitted through a puppy application
  • Information relating to your household, family, pets, living arrangements, lifestyle, and preferences when voluntarily provided as part of a puppy application
  • Information relating to a puppy inquiry, reservation, deposit, purchase, or adoption
  • Communications you send to us
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We ask that you not provide highly sensitive personal information unless it is specifically requested and necessary for your transaction with us.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically through cookies, server logs, analytics technologies, and similar tools.

This information may include:

  • Internet Protocol address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referring website
  • Approximate geographic location derived from an IP address
  • Date and time of visits
  • Interactions with our website
  • Cookie and similar identifiers

We may use this information to operate our website, understand how visitors use it, improve its performance, maintain security, and evaluate our marketing efforts.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, tags, analytics tools, and similar technologies.

Some cookies may be necessary for the website to function properly. Other technologies may help us understand website traffic, remember preferences, measure advertising performance, or improve our services.

Depending upon the technology used on our website and applicable law, visitors may be provided with options for managing nonessential cookies or tracking technologies.

You may also be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the operation or functionality of portions of the website.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Review puppy applications
  • Communicate with prospective and current puppy families
  • Determine whether one of our puppies may be an appropriate fit for an applicant
  • Manage puppy reservations, deposits, purchases, and related records
  • Provide information about available or upcoming litters
  • Send requested communications and updates
  • Provide customer service
  • Maintain our business records
  • Improve our website and services
  • Understand website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal rights
  • Carry out other purposes disclosed when information is collected

5. Puppy Applications

Information submitted through a Stony Point Doodles puppy application is used primarily to evaluate prospective puppy homes, communicate with applicants, understand the environment in which a puppy may live, and help us determine an appropriate puppy placement.

Submission of an application does not guarantee approval, reservation, or availability of a puppy.

We do not use information submitted in a puppy application to make decisions concerning employment, credit, insurance, housing, or other purposes governed by consumer reporting laws.

6. Payments

If you make a deposit or payment electronically, payment processing may be handled by a third party payment processor.

We may receive information concerning the transaction, such as your name, contact information, payment status, transaction amount, and transaction identifier. We generally do not need to directly store complete payment card information when payments are processed by a third party payment provider.

The payment provider’s collection and processing of information is also governed by its own privacy practices.

7. How We Share Information

We may disclose personal information to service providers and other parties that assist us in operating our business, such as:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Website developers and administrators
  • Email and communications providers
  • Payment processors
  • Customer management or form providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising and marketing technology providers, if used
  • Accountants, attorneys, insurers, and other professional advisors
  • Government agencies or other parties when disclosure is required or permitted by law

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect Stony Point Doodles, our customers, our animals, our property, or the rights and safety of others.

If the ownership or operation of Stony Point Doodles changes, information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, sale, restructuring, or transfer of business assets.

8. Sale and Sharing of Personal Information

Stony Point Doodles does not operate as a data broker and does not sell customer or puppy applicant information to third parties in exchange for money.

Certain advertising, analytics, or website technologies may constitute “sharing,” “targeted advertising,” or a similar activity under the privacy laws of certain states depending upon how those technologies are configured.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide consumers with the ability to exercise applicable choices concerning these activities.

9. Email and Marketing Communications

If you provide your email address or telephone number, we may use it to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you regarding Stony Point Doodles, our puppies, upcoming litters, applications, reservations, or related services.

If we send promotional email communications, you may unsubscribe by following the unsubscribe instructions contained in the message or by contacting us.

Transactional and administrative communications relating to an application, reservation, purchase, contract, payment, or other existing relationship may still be sent when appropriate.

10. Text Messages

If Stony Point Doodles offers text message communications, we will use telephone numbers for communications relating to inquiries, applications, puppy availability, reservations, appointments, or other communications you have requested or authorized.

Where consent is required for marketing text messages, we will obtain the appropriate consent before sending such messages.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing a puppy or any other product or service.

Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of marketing text messages by replying STOP or following other opt out instructions provided with the message.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including maintaining business and transaction records, administering puppy contracts and health records, resolving disputes, complying with legal obligations, and protecting our legal rights.

Retention periods may vary depending upon the nature of the information and our legal or business obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete, destroy, or anonymize it as appropriate.

12. Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No website, database, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we take reasonable precautions to protect personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending upon where you reside and the privacy laws applicable to Stony Point Doodles, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include the ability to:

  • Request information about personal information we maintain about you
  • Request access to certain personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Request information concerning certain disclosures of personal information
  • Opt out of certain forms of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising when applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based upon consent
  • Appeal certain decisions concerning a privacy request where applicable
  • Exercise privacy rights without unlawful discrimination

Not every right applies in every jurisdiction or to every business.

Even where a particular state privacy statute does not apply to Stony Point Doodles, we may attempt to honor reasonable requests to access, correct, or delete information when practical and consistent with our legal and business obligations.

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a privacy request.

14. State Specific Privacy Rights

Residents of certain states may have additional privacy rights under applicable state law.

If a state privacy law applies to Stony Point Doodles and provides you with additional rights, we will process valid requests in accordance with that law.

Where required, authorized agents may submit requests on behalf of consumers subject to appropriate verification and authorization procedures.

Stony Point Doodles will not unlawfully discriminate against a consumer for exercising applicable privacy rights.

15. Global Privacy Control and Browser Signals

Some browsers and devices provide privacy preference signals, including Global Privacy Control, or GPC.

Where applicable law requires Stony Point Doodles to recognize a legally valid browser based opt out preference signal, we will endeavor to process that signal as required by applicable law.

Please note that other browser based “Do Not Track” signals do not currently operate according to a universally accepted industry standard.

16. Children’s Privacy

Stony Point Doodles’ website and services are intended for adults interested in learning about or acquiring puppies and are not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information online from children under 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information directly from a child under 13 without appropriate parental consent when such consent is required, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information.

A parent or legal guardian who believes that a child has provided personal information to us may contact us using the information provided below.

17. Third Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to social media platforms, payment services, veterinarians, pet related resources, or other third party websites and services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third party websites or services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

18. Social Media

If you interact with Stony Point Doodles through a social media platform, information may be collected by both Stony Point Doodles and the applicable social media provider.

Your interaction with the social media provider is governed by that provider’s privacy policies and settings.

19. Data Breach Notification

If personal information maintained by Stony Point Doodles is involved in a security incident, we will investigate the incident and provide notifications to affected individuals, government authorities, or other parties when required by applicable law.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our business practices, website technologies, services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date shown at the top of this policy.

We encourage visitors to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Questions about this Privacy Policy, our privacy practices, or requests concerning personal information may be directed to:

Stony Point Doodles

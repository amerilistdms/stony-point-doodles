Our Guardian Home Program

A loving home for our future moms

Overview

What is a guardian home?

A guardian family raises and loves one of our selected female dogs as a permanent member of their household.

Stony Point Doodles retains the agreed breeding rights during her breeding career. Once she retires from breeding, she remains with her guardian family as their family pet, with full ownership transferred under the guardian agreement.

At Stony Point Doodles, we believe every dog deserves to grow up surrounded by love, attention, and the comforts of family life. Our Guardian Home Program gives a select family the opportunity to welcome one of our future breeding females into their home while helping us raise happy, healthy Bernedoodle puppies.

How the process works

From welcome home to retirement

  1. 01
    Welcome your puppy home

    Your puppy lives with you full time. You provide daily love, feeding, exercise, socialization, training, and routine veterinary care. We stay in touch and arrange any required breeding health screenings.

  2. 02
    Let us know when she comes into heat

    You notify us as soon as you see signs of a heat cycle and help ensure she is kept safely away from intact males. We coordinate reproductive appointments, testing, and breeding. She may visit or stay with us briefly during this period.

  3. 03
    Enjoy most of her pregnancy at home

    After breeding, she generally returns to your home for most of her pregnancy. We cover breeding related veterinary care and coordinate monitoring, nutrition guidance, and updates.

  4. 04
    She comes to us to have her puppies

    Usually around day 50 to 55 of pregnancy, she returns to Stony Point Doodles to settle in before delivery. She stays with us for whelping, nursing, and weaning, generally about 9 to 11 weeks in total, depending on her needs and veterinary guidance.

  5. 05
    She returns to her family

    Once the puppies are weaned and she is ready, she returns home to you. After her agreed breeding career ends, we arrange retirement and spaying as outlined in the agreement.

Who does what

Care, support, and shared responsibility

Guardian family

What does the guardian family provide?

A safe, stable, loving home; consistent care and training; routine veterinary visits; prompt communication about health or heat cycles; transportation or coordination for agreed appointments; and a commitment to preventing accidental breeding. We also encourage occasional visits so she feels comfortable with us before her first litter.

Stony Point Doodles

What does Stony Point Doodles provide?

We manage breeding decisions, reproductive testing, pregnancy related veterinary care, delivery, and puppy raising. We provide guidance throughout the process and coordinate the timing of each visit or stay. Your dog’s health and wellbeing always come first.

Program details

Program terms and compensation

We anticipate offering approved guardian families a puppy at no purchase cost, with a possible $500 thank you payment for each successful litter. Our proposed program allows up to three litters, only when medically appropriate. We prefer families who live within approximately 30 to 45 minutes of us. Final eligibility, compensation, ownership details, and responsibilities will be specified in a written agreement before placement.

Working together

A commitment built on trust

We understand that having your dog stay with us for several weeks while she raises puppies is a meaningful commitment. We will communicate openly, provide updates, and work to make each transition as comfortable as possible. We are looking for families who love dogs, value responsible breeding, and are excited to be part of the Stony Point Doodles family.

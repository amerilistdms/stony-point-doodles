Where Are They Now?

From their first little steps in our home to the adventures they share with their forever families, our puppies will always be part of the Stony Point Doodles family.

Forever families

Still part of our family

This page is dedicated to our alumni and the wonderful families who have welcomed them into their lives. We love seeing how our puppies grow, hearing about their personalities, and sharing the special moments that make each one unique.

Whether they are enjoying their first snowfall, celebrating a birthday, making new friends, or simply relaxing in their favorite spot at home, every update reminds us why we love raising puppies.

Have a Stony Point Doodles puppy? We would love to hear from you. Send us your favorite photos and updates so we can share your pup’s adventures with our growing Doodle family.

Once a Stony Point Doodle, always a Stony Point Doodle.

Meet our dogs

The pack that makes Stony Point feel like home.

Our breeding dogs are our pets first. They share the couch, the yard, and everyday family life with us.

The dogs behind Stony Point

Mila, Nico & Remy

The Mama

Mila

Mila is not simply one of our breeding dogs. She is part of our family. Affectionate, loving, intelligent, and happiest when she is close to her people.

The Proud Papa

Nico

Nico brings personality everywhere he goes. Beautiful coloring, an affectionate temperament, and a happy heart.

Chief Puppy Supervisor

Remy

Remy has not participated in the breeding plan yet, but he is certainly part of the family, and occasionally convinced that everything involves him.

The rest of the family

It takes a village to raise a doodle.

Mila, Nico and Remy get a lot of the attention. They are definitely not the only four-legged personalities running the household.

Yorkie · The Tiny Boss

Chai

Easily the smallest member of the family. Just don’t tell her that.

Shorkie · The Matriarch

Lulu

Grandma of the house. She watches the younger dogs with an expression that says, “I would have handled that differently.”

Maltipoo · The Sweet One

Loving and affectionate, but once she and Remy get going, the rest of the house usually isn’t far behind.

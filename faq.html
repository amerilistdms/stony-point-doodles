Questions

A few things families ask

If something isn’t here, send a note. We’re happy to talk it through.

Where are your puppies raised?

Every Stony Point puppy is raised inside our home, with our family — not in a kennel.

Are you a kennel?

No. We are a small, family-based breeding program. Our breeding dogs are our pets first.

How many litters do you have?

We keep the program small and only plan litters when the timing and pairing feel right.

How do I reserve a puppy?

Start with our application. Approved families may then place a deposit to secure their position on the selection list.

How is puppy selection order determined?

Selection order is based on application approval and the order in which deposits are received.

When do families select their puppy?

We let them grow first. We also want puppies to receive their initial veterinary evaluation before selections begin. Health always comes first.

Can I choose based on color?

Absolutely — appearance is part of it. We also encourage families to consider personality, temperament, energy, and overall fit.

Can I visit the puppies?

Because the health of young puppies matters so much, visits may be limited in their earliest weeks. We’ll share visit details when the time is right.

Do you stay in touch after the puppy goes home?

We certainly hope so. We love receiving updates. Puppy pickup does not have to be the end of our relationship.

Still curious?

We’re happy to talk it through.
